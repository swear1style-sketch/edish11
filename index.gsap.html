Before we begin

Have specific architecture or staffing questions? Schedule a call and an engineering director will answer.

How quickly can an Edish Connect pod start committing code?

Most teams have senior engineers embedded in your standups and opening PRs within one to two weeks, following automated security vetting and repo onboarding.

How do you evaluate and select engineers for our roadmap?

We evaluate candidates through deep system design reviews, production debugging scenarios, and domain matching. We present only senior practitioners who've owned similar systems in production.

Who owns the intellectual property and code?

You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, architectural documentation, and infrastructure scripts from the first commit. Everything lives directly in your repositories.

How is Edish Connect different from traditional IT staffing?

Staffing agencies sell resumes and bill hours. We are an engineering growth company led by senior architects who provide technical oversight, architectural reviews, and delivery accountability.

Can we transition from dedicated teams to BOT or GCC?

Yes. Many partners begin with an embedded pod to accelerate a release, then expand into a dedicated ODC or full Build-Operate-Transfer center as product lines mature.

What does the initial architecture consultation involve?

You will speak directly with an engineering director, not an account executive. We will discuss your current architecture, bottlenecks, and roadmap to recommend the right model.
