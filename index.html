One step at a time

Most companies start small. Take on a few engineers, then move up a step when the work grows. Nobody has to commit to a whole centre on day one.

  1. Step 1

    A few engineers

    Staff augmentation · usually 4 to 10 people

    Good for a startup or a single project. The engineers join your team, use your tools and report to your leads.

  2. Step 2

    A dedicated team

    Dedicated team · often 15 or more

    Once the product takes off, it needs people who work on nothing else. We put that team together and keep it stable.

  3. Step 3

    A development centre

    ODC · often 50 or more

    Developers, QA and designers on your product full time, working from our office. We run the centre and manage the people.

  4. Step 4

    A capability centre

    GCC · more than engineering

    Your own company in India, with HR, finance, marketing or R&D next to engineering. You run it, and we help you set it up.

  5. Step 5

    Take it over

    BOT · build, operate, transfer

    We build the centre and run it until it's stable. Then the whole thing, people included, moves over to you.

Questions people ask us

Something else on your mind? Send it through the form below and someone from our team will reply.

What's the difference between an ODC and a GCC?

An ODC is a development centre: engineers working on your software, run and managed by us, usually from our office. A GCC is bigger and it's yours. Next to engineering it can have HR, finance, marketing, legal or R&D, and you run it. We help you set it up.

We only need seven or eight engineers. Is that too small?

No. That's staff augmentation, and it's how a lot of startups begin with us. You don't need an ODC or a GCC for a small team.

Do we have to come to India to set up an ODC?

No. Most ODCs are set up over video and phone calls. The team works from our premises, so you don't need an office of your own here.

Can we move from one model to another later?

Yes. Teams often start with a few engineers, grow into a dedicated team and then an ODC. If you later want the centre under your own name, we can hand it over to you. That's BOT.

Do you only work in Jaipur?

No. Jaipur is the example we use most, but the right city depends on the skills you need and the culture you want around your team. We might suggest Indore, Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam or somewhere else.

What happens after I send the form?

Our team reads your requirement and contacts you by email or phone. After a call or two, we send a detailed proposal. Payment is agreed separately once you're happy with it, and nothing is paid through this website.
